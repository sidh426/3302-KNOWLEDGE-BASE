## Living with one car for years

I've had my 440i Gran Coupe since I was 16 and I've put about 90,000 miles on it. I'm not a professional mechanic, so this page is just what I've learned as an owner. BMWs have a reputation for being expensive to keep up, and in my experience that's partly true, but a lot of the cost comes down to whether you pay BMW prices or find smarter ways to do the same job.

## Routine maintenance

Most of what I've done is the normal wear and tear that comes with a performance car:

- **Oil changes:** a turbocharged engine is hard on oil, and many owners change it sooner than the car's service indicator suggests.
- **Other fluids:** coolant, brake fluid, and the rest, on a regular schedule.
- **Spark plugs:** turbo engines wear plugs faster than a naturally aspirated one.
- **Brakes:** pads and rotors wear out sooner on a car that's driven spiritedly.
- **Tires:** I go through tires fairly often, which is part of the cost of a car that grips and gets driven hard.

### Doing the work myself

Recently I started doing more of the work on my own. I've changed the spark plugs and replaced the engine mounts. Spark plugs are a common first job for owners who want to start working on their own cars. Engine mounts are a bigger job, and they matter because worn ones let the engine move around and send vibration into the cabin. Doing the work myself saves labor costs, and it means I get to know the car a lot better than I would by only dropping it off at a shop.

## The big repair: AC condenser

Somewhere around 60,000 to 70,000 miles my AC condenser failed. This is a bigger deal than it sounds in Houston, where the AC isn't optional. The BMW-branded part was very expensive, so I bought a condenser from Denso instead, a major global supplier of automotive parts, and had an independent shop install it instead of going through BMW. That choice avoided the much higher price of the BMW part.

> Lesson learned: for an out-of-warranty BMW, a good independent shop and quality aftermarket parts can make ownership far more affordable.
